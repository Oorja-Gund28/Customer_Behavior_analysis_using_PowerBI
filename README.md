# E-commerce Customer Behavior Analysis (Power BI)

This Power BI report analyzes **customer behavior** of an e-commerce platform, based on data containing multiple user events.  
The data covers a duration of **2 months**, provided in 2 tables:  
- `Sales_Data_Ecommerce`
- `Promotion`

I used **Power BI Desktop** to extract and load the data, and **Power Query Editor** to transform it.  

The report focuses on the following themes:  
- (a) Overview of various KPIs  
- (b) Pricing and fluctuations  
- (c) Promotion and its effects  
- (d) Search and recommendations  

---

## Insights from the Analysis

### Basis Data Cleaning Questions

- **What variables can be derived out of the data which can be helpful?**  
  - Revenue, Potential Revenue, and Discounted Revenue.

- **Who can use this dashboard?**  
  - Sales team: To track the impact of discounts on sales.  
  - Marketing team: To monitor engagement trends.  
  - Product Managers: To optimize pricing strategies.  
  - Finance team: To evaluate effects on revenue.

- **What value would be generated by using this dashboard?**  
  - Enhances pricing strategies, boosts customer engagement, and helps allocate budgets more effectively.

- **What additional data can be helpful to add more insights and value to the team?**  
  - Data showing search behavior of customers — whether *brand by category* or *category by brand*, and profit margins.

---

### Business Questions & Findings

1. **How is price varying by brand/category/time/channel?**  
   - Electronics category and MSI brand have the highest average price, while Stationery category and Vitek brand have the lowest.  
   - Average price peaks at 2200 hrs.

2. **Is traffic varied by day/time/channel?**  
   - Yes — traffic is highest on Fridays between 1400–1600 hrs, and is almost the same across both channels.

3. **Define and calculate high-level metrics (like Revenue, Potential Revenue, etc.) by month, state, etc.**  
   - Total Revenue: **5.65M**  
   - Potential Revenue: **12.95M**  
   - Total Categories: **12**  
   - Total Brands: **50**  
   - Revenue is highest on Sundays at 1100 hrs and in the month of November.  
   - Filled map displays distribution of categories, subcategories, brands, and revenue across states.

4. **What is activity by brand or category, brand preference, brand activity by various parameters, etc.?**  
   - Electronics category and Apple brand have the highest revenue.  
   - Samsung is the most preferred brand.  
   - Apple gave the maximum discount value.

5. **How is the search behavior — brand search by category / category search by brand?**  
   - Data is insufficient to answer this question.

6. **What is the effect of special promotions?**  
   - Most discounts are offered in the Electronics category, which also has the highest revenue.  
   - Higher discount count correlates with increased brand preference (Samsung being most preferred).

7. **How are pricing fluctuations affecting sales?**  
   - Lower average price is leading to higher revenue — hence Sunday sees the highest revenue.

8. **Miscellaneous:**  
   - Funnel chart shows how purchase types are funneled out from view and cart.  
   - 100% stacked chart shows % of discounted product revenue in total revenue — Electronics leads.  
   - Tree map shows subcategory-wise revenue — Smartphones top the Electronics category.  
   - Pie chart shows number of user sessions by category — Electronics has the highest engagement.
